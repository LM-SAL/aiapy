`aiapy.calibrate.register` now uses `reproject` for image registration. Full-resolution images are returned as 4096 by 4096 pixels, and cutouts are placed on the north-aligned full-disk pixel grid with bounds that contain their transformed field of view. The ``method`` argument, including the CuPy backend, has been removed; use ``algorithm`` and ``order`` to select the reprojection algorithm and interpolation order.
